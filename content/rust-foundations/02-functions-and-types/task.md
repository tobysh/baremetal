Write two functions:

- `fn celsius_to_fahrenheit(c: f64) -> f64` converts degrees Celsius to Fahrenheit (`c * 9/5 + 32`).
- `fn is_leap_year(year: u32) -> bool` returns `true` for a leap year: divisible by 4, except years divisible by 100, unless also divisible by 400. So 2000 and 2024 are leap years; 1900 and 2023 are not.
