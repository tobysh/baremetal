Read stdin as a program (at most 300 lines). Lines are numbered from 1 and include blank lines and `#` comments, which are ignored. A statement is either `name = expr` or a sink call `sink_sql(arg)` / `sink_exec(arg)`. An expr is one or more operands joined by ` + ` (space, plus, space); an operand is a variable name, a double-quoted literal (no ` + ` and no quote inside) or a call `f(arg)` / `f()` where the arg is a variable or literal. `input()` and `env()` give mask 3, `escape_sql(x)` gives mask(x) with bit 1 cleared, `quote_shell(x)` gives mask(x) with bit 2 cleared, any other call gives mask of its argument (0 with no argument). For every sink print `line <N>: <sink>(<arg>) TAINTED` if the argument's mask has the bit for that sink (1 for `sink_sql`, 2 for `sink_exec`), otherwise `line <N>: <sink>(<arg>) clean`. Print `no sinks` if there are none.
