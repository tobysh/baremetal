Implement:

- `void isort(long *a, size_t n)` sorts the signed array into ascending order, in place. `n` may be 0 or 1.
- `size_t count_inversions(const long *a, size_t n)` returns the number of pairs `i < j` with `a[i] > a[j]`, without modifying the array.

Arrays with negative numbers, duplicates and extreme values are tried.
