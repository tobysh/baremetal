Read `n`, then `n` records (`name score`), and print:

```
1. ada 91
2. linus 91
3. grace 78
average: 86.67
passed: 3 of 3
```

Rank lines are in descending score order, ties in input order. The average has two decimals. `passed` counts scores of 50 or more. With `n` of 0, print `average: 0.00` and `passed: 0 of 0`. Names are one word of at most 15 characters, and `n` is at most 20.
