Read a number from `1` to `7` for the day of the week (`1` is Monday). Print the day's name on one line, then `weekday` or `weekend` on the next. For input `6`:

```
Saturday
weekend
```

Days 1 to 5 are weekdays, 6 and 7 are the weekend. For any other number print just `invalid day`.

Use a `switch`. Several values can share the `weekday` code. Other inputs are tried, including `0`, `8` and negative numbers.
