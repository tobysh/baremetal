Write a program that reads whole numbers until the input ends, and then reports on them. There may be no numbers at all, or hundreds. They are separated by spaces or newlines.

The report is three labelled lines: how many numbers there were, what they add up to, and the largest. For the input `3 9 -2 7` it is:

```
count: 4
sum: 17
max: 9
```

The largest of nothing does not exist, so when the input holds no numbers, print the word `none` after `max:` (and zero for the other two).

Other inputs are tried, including lists of only negative numbers. Start from the empty file: choose your own variables and functions.
