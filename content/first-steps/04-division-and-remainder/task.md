The starter declares three numbers. Make the program print exactly:

```
4 hours 35 minutes
12 dollars and 34 cents
last digit of 1234: 4
```

Your program is also run with other starting numbers, so calculate the answers from the variables rather than typing them. Work the values out with `/` and `%` (the starter's `0`s are placeholders). 275 minutes is the first line; 1234 cents is the second; and the third is the last digit of 1234.
