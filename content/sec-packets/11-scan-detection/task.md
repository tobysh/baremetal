Read stdin (at most 1000 lines, at most 256 clients, at most 256 distinct targets per client; names at most 63 characters). A record is five whitespace-separated fields: an integer time (ignored), client, server, port (1 to 65535) and outcome (`open`, `closed` or `filtered`). Ignore every other line. For each client count distinct (server, port) targets, taking the first outcome seen for each target. Print `CLIENT targets=N open=O closed=C filtered=F` for every client with N at least 5 and `closed+filtered` greater than `open`, sorted by N descending then `strcmp` on the client. If none qualify, or the input is empty, print `no scans detected`. A line with more than five fields, fewer than five, a non-numeric time or any value outside the rules above is ignored entirely and counts as nothing; a client whose only valid records number fewer than five targets is simply not listed. A client with exactly 5 targets qualifies if `closed+filtered > open`; equal counts do not qualify.

Tests use several different inputs, including boundary counts, repeated targets, junk lines and ties. Keep the supplied function names and types.
