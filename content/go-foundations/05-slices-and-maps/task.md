Implement two functions:

- `WordCount(text string) map[string]int` counts how often each word occurs. Words are separated by whitespace and compared case-insensitively (`"Go go GO"` counts three of `"go"`). Empty text gives an empty (non-nil) map.
- `Dedup(nums []int) []int` returns the numbers with duplicates removed, keeping the first occurrence of each and the original order. The input must not be modified. An empty input gives an empty result.
