Read stdin (at most 500 lines, each at most 1000 characters). A line is well formed when: it has a first whitespace-separated token (the address, at most 63 characters); it contains a pair of double quotes enclosing exactly three whitespace-separated words (method, path, version; paths are at most 500 characters); and after the closing quote come exactly two tokens, a status in 100 to 599 and a size that is `-` or digits. Print the first line `2xx=A 3xx=B 4xx=C 5xx=D malformed=M` (1xx lines are well formed but not counted; empty lines are malformed). Then, for each address with two or more well-formed requests whose lowercased path contains `../`, `%2e%2e` or `/etc/passwd` (count each request once), print `SUSPECT ADDRESS hits=N`, sorted by hits descending and then by `strcmp` on the address.

Tests use several different logs. Keep the supplied function names and types.
