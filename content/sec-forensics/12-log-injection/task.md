Implement `size_t log_escape(char *out,size_t cap,const char *in)`. Walk `in` (NULL means the empty string) byte by byte. `\n` becomes the two characters `\n`, `\r` becomes `\r`, a backslash becomes two backslashes, any other byte below 0x20 or equal to 0x7f becomes `\x` plus two lowercase hex digits, and every other byte (including 0x80 and above) is copied. Return the total length of the escaped text, excluding the terminating zero, whether or not it fit. Write only whole escapes: stop writing at the first escape that does not fit while leaving room for the terminating zero, and write nothing further even if a later, shorter piece would fit. If `cap>0` always terminate the output; if `cap==0` or `out` is NULL write nothing.

Tests sweep capacities and check bytes around the buffer. Keep the supplied function names and types.
